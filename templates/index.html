Website Connections

The website talks to these endpoints.

EndpointUsed for
POST /api/userCreate an account
POST /api/authenticateSign in
DELETE /api/authenticateSign out (also for new accounts waiting for approval)
GET /api/idWho is signed in
GET / PUT /api/fops/progress/<game>Saved progress for Spot the Scam, Bingo, and Movement Games
POST /api/fops/bingo/roomsStart a Bingo game with friends
POST /api/fops/bingo/rooms/<code>/joinJoin a Bingo game by its number
POST /api/fops/bingo/rooms/<code>/startHost starts the shared caller
POST /api/fops/bingo/rooms/<code>/claimClaim BINGO (checked by the server)
GET /api/fops/profile/meWho is signed in, including new accounts waiting for approval
GET / PUT /api/fops/profile/interestsA member's interest survey on their profile (works right after signup)
GET /api/fops/profile/interests/summarySurvey totals for planning events (Admin or Volunteer, no names)
