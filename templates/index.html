Friends of Poway Seniors Backend

The Flask server behind the Friends of Poway Seniors website: accounts, sign-in, saved progress, Bingo rooms, and the interest survey.

What does the backend do?

  1. Stores member accounts with hashed passwords and signs people in with a secure cookie.
  2. New accounts stay inactive until an admin verifies them (OCS account verification).
  3. Saves each game's progress to the member's account, so seniors can practice on any computer.

Which APIs does the website call?

POST /api/fops/bingo/roomsStart, join, and claim Bingo
GET /api/fops/profile/meWho is signed in, even while waiting for approval
GET/PUT /api/fops/profile/interestsInterest survey (works right after signup)
GET /api/fops/profile/interests/summarySurvey totals (Admin or Volunteer)

What data do we keep?

  1. Users, sections, and personas from the OCS Flask template.
  2. Game progress (Spot the Scam, Bingo, Movement Games) as JSON per member.
  3. Interest survey answers, summed without names to help volunteers plan events.
  4. Planned next: events, RSVPs, and scam reports.
